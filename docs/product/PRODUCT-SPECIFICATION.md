# Product Specification

## Objective
Proactively monitor approved regulatory sources, structure publications, support human-controlled CRU/CRMP assessment, track controls/gaps/remediation and retain complete audit evidence.

## Personas
Compliance Officer, Risk Champion, Compliance Head, Business Unit Head, Action Owner, Internal Auditor, Application Administrator and Support Operator.

## Modules
Regulatory Radar; publications/versions; extraction and human validation; obligations/applicability; CRU; CRMP/remediation; assignment/SLA; evidence/audit; dashboards/exports; administration.

## Rules
AI is advisory; suggestions and human-confirmed values remain distinct; missing facts remain unconfirmed; decisions occur in the authenticated app; material changes reopen approval; original versions are never overwritten; closure requires configured evidence and validation.
