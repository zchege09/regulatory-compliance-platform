# SLA and Escalation Matrix

Initial configurable examples requiring Compliance approval:

| Event | Timing | Target |
|---|---:|---|
| Early reminder | Due -10 business days | Owner |
| Reminder | Due -5 business days | Owner |
| Warning | Due -2 business days | Owner |
| Due | Due date | Owner |
| Overdue | Due +1 business day | Owner and workflow manager |
| Escalation 1 | Due +3 business days | Manager/role |
| Escalation 2 | Due +5 business days | Compliance Head/approved role |

Use approved business calendar, prevent duplicates, preserve due-date history and audit every notification/escalation.
