# SCR-007: Risk Champion Review

- **Primary roles:** Risk Champion
- **Sections/data:** Assessment snapshot and business impact
- **Actions:** Confirm or return

## UX and control requirements
Show current workflow state, owner, due date and regulatory ID. Hide unavailable actions for usability but always enforce authorization server-side. Display validation errors at field/summary level. Material field changes must warn the user and follow reapproval rules. Relevant actions create audit events. Add wireframe and field-to-API mapping before build.
