
## Detailed functional design gate
Do not implement a feature from an ADR alone. Verify the linked journey, workflow, screen, field rules, permission, exception/runbook, acceptance criteria and tests. If detail is missing or conflicting, update/approve the product specification before coding rather than inventing behaviour.
